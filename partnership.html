04 — Questions

Asked and answered.

What does a partnership include?

Placement across the kart, suit and helmet; co-branded content with monthly reporting; paddock hospitality at British Championship rounds; and activation support through the season. Exact scope is set by tier and confirmed in writing.

Can we start with a single event?

Yes. One-off activations — a race weekend, a store appearance, a content series — are available outside the tier programme. If you want to test the platform before committing to a season, that's a sensible way in.

How is return measured?

Monthly reporting on reach and engagement, media placements logged, and access to every event your brand appears at. If the numbers matter, they're shown — without being asked.
