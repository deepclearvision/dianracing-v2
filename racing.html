02 — PFI · Grantham — Nov 2025

The weekend that made a champion.

Dhian arrived at the final round of the Wera Tools British Kart Championships fifteen points behind the championship leader — with a title on the line and a full season of work behind him.

He opened with two commanding heat wins. Then Saturday's final delivered the kind of blow that ends seasons: a technical disqualification wiped out the points. Sunday morning, he had to win everything — and he did.

The championship came down to the last lap of the last race. Dhian took the lead with a late overtake, won the final, and took the British title by a single point.

04 — Sakhir · Bahrain — Dec 2025

Team GB. The Rotax Grand Finals.

In December 2025, Dhian wore Great Britain's colours at the Rotax Max Challenge Grand Finals in Bahrain — the biggest prize in international karting. Four hundred drivers. More than sixty nations. One world final. He started deep in the field and charged from P15 to the lead inside three laps — the kind of move that wins world finals.
